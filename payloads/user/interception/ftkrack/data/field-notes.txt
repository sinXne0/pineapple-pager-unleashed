FTKRACK field notes
===================

Operator checklist
------------------
1. Confirm written authorization for the target AP and clients.
2. Put the MT7921 into radiotap monitor mode (wlan1mon, ARPHRD 803) from the
   Pineapple UI. FTKRACK does not change interface mode.
3. Lock wlan1mon to the channel used by the target 802.11r AP.
4. Launch payload.sh; authorize in INTRO; verify MON=OK in TARGET.
5. In CAPTURE, sniff while a client performs an FT transition. You need an
   FT Reassociation Request carrying MDIE (54) and FTIE (55).
6. In REPLAY, inject the captured frame (default 3 times).
7. In PROOF, verify. Require CONFIRMED with: accepted writes, status-0
   reassociation response, and CCMP PN reuse/regression.

What a negative result means
----------------------------
- No FT request: AP may not use 802.11r, or traffic was on a different channel.
- No response after replay: AP patched, PMF required, or frame did not reach it.
- No PN reuse: AP patched for CVE-2017-13082, or not enough AP data captured.
- Write errors: monitor interface does not accept injected management frames.

Do not interpret UNCONFIRMED as a successful attack.

Frame reference
---------------
Reassociation Request (management type 0, subtype 2):
  FC(2) Duration(2) Addr1=AP(6) Addr2=STA(6) Addr3=BSSID(6) Seq(2)
  Capability(2) Listen(2) CurrentAP(6) IEs...
FTIE (ID 55):
  MIC Control(2) MIC(16) SNonce(32) ANonce(32) [subelements...]
MDIE (ID 54):
  MDID(2) FT Capability/Policy(1)
CCMP header (8) after the MAC/QoS header:
  PN0 PN1 res0 key_id/ext_iv PN2 PN3 PN4 PN5
  PN = PN0 | PN1<<8 | PN2<<16 | PN3<<24 | PN4<<32 | PN5<<40
